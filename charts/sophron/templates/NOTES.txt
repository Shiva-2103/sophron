Sophron is starting in {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

1. Open the dashboard:
   kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "sophron.fullname" . }} 8081:{{ .Values.service.port }}
   then http://localhost:8081

2. Sign in as admin:
   kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get secret {{ include "sophron.authSecret" . }} -o jsonpath='{.data.password}' | base64 -d; echo

3. Check the setup (data sources, prices, Slack, fix permissions): the Setup page.
